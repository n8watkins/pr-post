## The midweek update

- `report_md`: the update body in Markdown, without the title line (the title is added for you).
  The whole update, title included, is at most 120 words.
  Use exactly this format:

```markdown
- 2 to 4 bullets, at most 25 words each, biggest first, each with a Markdown link to the item in the brief.
- The first bullet must stand alone: it is this person's line in the email.
```

  If nothing notable happened (only version bumps, chores or formatting), the body is the single line `Quiet since <the window's start>.`
  For a person marked as the reader, write in the second person ("you shipped...").
